Join the lab

Learn enzyme chemistry by doing it.

The Vuksanovic Lab welcomes motivated Hofstra undergraduates interested in biochemistry, mechanistic enzymology, structural biology, and microbial glycobiology.

Undergraduate research

Students in the lab will engage with questions at the interface of chemistry and biology, with an emphasis on understanding how enzymes recognize substrates and control challenging chemical transformations.

If you are interested in research with the group, email Dr. Vuksanovic and briefly introduce yourself, your scientific interests, and any relevant coursework or research experience.
