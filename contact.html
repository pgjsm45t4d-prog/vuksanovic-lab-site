Lab culture

A place to learn and grow.

The Vuksanovic Lab is committed to a respectful, supportive, and welcoming research environment. We welcome students with a wide range of backgrounds, experiences, perspectives, and paths into science.

You do not need to arrive with research experience or a perfectly defined career plan. Curiosity, a willingness to learn, and respect for the people around you matter most.

We value kindness, collaboration, thoughtful questions, and growth. Mistakes are part of learning, and everyone should have a fair opportunity to develop confidence, independence, and a sense of belonging in science.
