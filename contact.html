Lab culture

A place to learn, contribute, and grow.

The Vuksanovic Lab is committed to building a respectful, supportive, and inclusive research environment. We welcome students of all backgrounds, identities, experiences, and paths into science, including those who may not yet see themselves represented in research.

We value curiosity, kindness, collaboration, and growth. Questions are encouraged, mistakes are part of learning, and everyone should have the opportunity to contribute ideas, develop confidence, and grow as a scientist.
